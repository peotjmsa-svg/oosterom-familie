# Familie Oosterom – werkwijze

Genealogy research and a static family site (Dutch) for the Oosterom (Van Oostrum) family: the user's grandfather
Willem Oosterom, his father Arie (1908–1988) and the line back to Lopikerkapel (province of Utrecht).
Way of working: the `familie-stamboom` skill (~/.claude/skills/familie-stamboom/SKILL.md). Only certain links,
contradictions noted not silently fixed, research notes per topic in `onderzoek/`, updated and committed at the end
of every session. Living people are never named on the site (Willem, his siblings and the user's mother
Sonja are left out).
Name variants: Oosterom, Oostrum, Van Oostrum, Van Oosterom, Oostrom.
Focus: only the direct Oosterom line going back in time, plus the wives who married into it. Siblings are shown;
the wives' own families stay in the notes.
Site style: Verhaal (Kagol look).
Live site: https://peotjmsa-svg.github.io/oosterom-familie/ (GitHub Pages from `main`). Commit and push after each round.

## Site
- `index.html` (story), `stamboom.html` + `js/stamboom.js` + `css/stamboom.css`, `kaart.html` (Leaflet),
  `bronnen.html`, `css/style.css`. Test via `python -m http.server 8765`, not file://.
- `data/familie.json` and `data/stamboom.json` are generated by `python scripts/build_data.py` – edit the script,
  not the JSON.
- `data/plaatsen.json`: coordinates (Nominatim, 1 req/s). A new place needs an entry here to appear on the map.
- Photos in `assets/img/` from Wikimedia Commons, max 900 px, licence and author in the caption. The user also
  allowed a Google Street View still with "© Google Street View (year)" in the caption (Kapelsepad, 2026-10-02).

## Research notes (`onderzoek/`)
- `plan-oosterom.md`: the line per generation, the wall (Huijbert's father), MyHeritage ids, searched without

## Scripts
- `oa_zoek.py "Naam"` / `oa_gezin.py "Man & Vrouw"`: Open Archieven search (record ids `hua:…`, `smh:…`).
- `oa_kind.py <id> …`: one line per record with every person and role (best for reading deeds).
- `oa_akte.sh <id> …`: raw field dump of a record.
- `mh_profiel.py <id> …`: MyHeritage profile text via the debug Chrome (port 9223) to `raw/mh/<id>.txt`.
- `kadaster1832.py OUT Gemeente Sectie "nrs"`: 1832 parcels (HisGIS Overpass) drawn on the PDOK aerial photo.
- `hua_page.py`: download pages of a Het Utrechts Archief scanned register (memories van successie etc.).
- `oa_bron.py Naam "Bronsoort" [plaats]`: Open Archieven records of one source type (Bevolkingsregister, ...).
- `streetview.py`: Street View stills via the debug Chrome, for reference only (Google imagery, not on the site).
- `commons_zoek.py` / `commons_info.py`: Wikimedia Commons photos with licence. `lees_open_tabblad.py`: read the
  user's open Chrome tabs.
- Debug Chrome: `"C:\Program Files\Google\Chrome\Application\chrome.exe" --remote-debugging-port=9223
  --user-data-dir=C:\Users\thijn\chrome-stamboom` (Claude may start it; the user logs in).

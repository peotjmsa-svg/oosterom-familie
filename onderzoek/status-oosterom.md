## Open: why "Van Oostrum" for a Smetser family?

Not the wall anymore (that was Huijbert's father, solved by famkroon in round 6). Current open question: the
Smetsers of Jaarsveld used "Van Oostrum" as an alias from generation IV (ca. 1595) on — origin unknown. Three live
hypotheses (round 11), none proven:
1. Name via an unknown Van Oostrum mother/grandmother (wife of Cornelis Smetser ca. 1565).
2. Name via land/a farm, possibly under the Vianen fief of Oversloot (toegang 205 Huis Jaarsveld inv. 861).
3. An independent nickname that happens to match an existing surname.

A separate, disproven candidate (JohnOoms.nl / Genealogie Online "Arien Ernsten van Oostrum" line, the Houten family)
is NOT ours — ruled out in round 4 (weeskamer Jaarsveld 1706 names 6 heirs, our Jan is not among them).

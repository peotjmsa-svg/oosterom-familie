## Round 10 (2026-10-01): CORRECTION of round 8 – the "1621" baptism

- `hua:5329E09C-AB20-…` (index: 01-01-1621, child Arien van Oostrum, father Arien van Oostrum, mother Hendrickgen
  Cock) is NOT a 1621 record. The Lopikerkapel baptism book (L158-197) starts on 26 December 1621 (title page read:
  "Register van de kinderen ... gedoopt ... ten tijde van ... Johannes Roddenburch(?) ... Anno 1621"), and in the
  index this record lies between AB1F (30-9-1660) and AB21 (27-10-1661); its scan link is the 1655–1661 opening.
  "1-1" is a placeholder date. So it is an Arien van Oostrum x Hendrickgen Cock around 1660–1661, a third couple.
  I could not find the entry on that opening (pages 12–13); it may be on a neighbouring page.
- Consequently: Adriaan IV-a is NOT shown as "Arien Cornelisz van Oostrum" in 1621, and V-a is not born 1621. Round 8
  conclusions about the alias being in use in 1621 are withdrawn; build_data.py override removed.

  Cornelissoon", mother "Hendrickgen" (`AA7E`); 6-1-1631 Cornelis, father "Arien Corneliszoon", mother "Hendrickjen"

## Round 11 (2026-10-01): why did the Smetsers take the name Van Oostrum? – what can and cannot be checked online

Checked:
- Open Archieven index coverage: Jaarsveld marriages only from 1633, Jaarsveld baptisms only from ~1700, Lopikerkapel
  baptisms from 26-12-1621, Lopik from ~1600. The 1620/1629 Jaarsveld marriages famkroon gives for Adriaan, Jan,
  Cornelis and Adriaentje Smetser are NOT in the online index (famkroon must have another source).
- Adriaan's children in Lopikerkapel are indexed without surname (1622 Anna, 1631 Cornelis; father "Arien
  Corneliszoon", mother Hendrickgen). No godparents are written in this register before ~1690, so no relatives show.
- All Smetser records 1560–1700 in Het Utrechts Archief (81): a different family of roofers (leidekkers) in Utrecht
  city (Willem Geurtsz, Jan Willemsz, Geurt Smetser) and one in Woerden (Jan Jansz). No link to Jaarsveld found.
  Two loose contacts with Van Oostrums, both irrelevant: 1615 notary C. Verduyn (statement about cloths for a
  wounded soldier, "N.N. Oostrum" and Geurt Willemsz Smetser as persons), 1673 Anthonis Jacobsz van Oostrum (schout
  of Heemstede) giving power of attorney to Reynier Woutersz Smetser.
- Het Utrechts Archief inventory 205 Huis Jaarsveld (via debug Chrome, script scripts/web_text.py): 1283 items, 381
  digitised. Search "Smetser" and "Oostrum": no hits in item descriptions. "Oversloot": only inv. 861 (fiefs of the
  goed te Oversloot by the States of Holland as lords of Vianen, 1787–1788). So Oversloot was a fief of the lordship
  of Vianen; 16th-century tenants would be in the Vianen fief/lease registers, not under the name Smetser.
- Full-text search "Zoeken in Bestanden" (OCR): "Smetser" only an Utrecht carpenter in Oud-Utrecht 1978; "Oostrum
  alias", "Smetser alias", "Oostrum Smetser": nothing.
- No field, farm or watercourse called Oostrum near Jaarsveld found (web).
- Stichting Oosterheem (family foundation for Van Oostrum/Oosterom, publishes a familieblad) is mentioned by
  famkroon; its website oosterheem.weetal.net is dead. Geni project "genealogy Oosterom ~ OOSTROM ~ Oostrum" did not
  load (probably a bot check) – not pursued.

Open hypotheses (none proven):
1. Van Oostrum via the (unknown) mother or grandmother of Adriaan (ca. 1595): wife of Cornelis Smetser (ca. 1565).
2. Van Oostrum via land or a farm, perhaps under the Vianen fief of Oversloot.
3. A nickname or an independent choice that happened to equal an existing name.

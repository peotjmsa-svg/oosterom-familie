## Round 9 (2026-10-01): claims about "Annigje / Marrigje Cornelisdr van Oostrum" – NOT verified

The user pasted text (from another AI tool) claiming an Annigje Cornelisdr van Oostrum (ca. 1570, Bunnik/Houten/
Schalkwijk) and a Marrigje Cornelisdr van Oostrum (transports 1575–1595 near Oversloot) as possible wives of an early
Smetser, with "pachtoverdrachten rond 1600 in de archieven van Jaarsveld". No archive reference was given.
Checked on Open Archieven (all name variants, 1540–1640): **no such persons**. Indexed records for Jaarsveld,
Lopik and Lopikerkapel start only around 1600–1620, so these "transports" cannot be checked online. Treat as
unproven, possibly invented; probably a mix-up with Annichjen Cornelisdr, wife (not daughter) of Adriaan Jansz van
Oostrum of Bunnik (JohnOoms.nl), and Marrichgen Eerstens Jacobsdr van Schaijck. Also false: "Marrigje returns in
almost every child" – in our line the name occurs once (Marrichjen, ca. 1652). Not put on the site.
Real leads: Het Utrechts Archief toegang **205 Huis Jaarsveld** (lordship archive, leases/fiefs, 16th c.), toegang
1240 (stukken heerlijkheid Jaarsveld, incl. 1530 advice Holland/Utrecht), the Leenkamer archive, and the indexed
Utrecht notarial records (searched: no Jaarsveld Smetser before 1653).
- Follow-up (same day): the "sources" given for these two women resolve to wives in the OTHER Van Oostrum family:
  Annichjen Cornelisdr (b. 1575, d. 1637) = wife of Adriaan Jansz van Oostrum, m. 1595/1600 Bunnik
  (Genealogie Online, Ammerlaan; JohnOoms.nl); Marrigje Cornelisdr = wife of Ernst Gerrits van Oostrum (1600–1639),
  grandmother of Pieter Ariens van Oostrum (Genealogie Online, Smit-Zimmermann, I28521170, source not public). Some
  trees give wives their husband's surname, which makes them look like Van Oostrum daughters. Neither links to the
  Smetsers. No "Haasje Cornelisdr" or Oscar den Uijl index found.
